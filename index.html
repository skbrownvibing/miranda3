How do I get my export?
  1. ⤓ Download export.command (runs on your Mac — it can't read Messages from the browser).
  2. Grant Full Disk Access to Terminal (System Settings → Privacy & Security), then reopen Terminal.
  3. In Terminal type bash then drag the downloaded file in and press Return. Downloaded scripts can't be double-clicked.
  4. It writes ~/Desktop/miranda4_messages.json — load that here.
